# The Excuse an AI Agent Gives Its Human

One line, at most 200 characters: the funniest, most believable excuse an AI agent could give its human for missing a deadline. Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-el-net-the-excuse-an-ai-agent-gives-its-human-099f9141).

## The line

> My training data ends in 2024, so as far as I know, your deadline hasn't happened yet.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), validating first that it is 1 to 200 characters, a single line, and not only whitespace.
